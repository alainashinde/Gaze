Frequently Asked Questions
Where is my data stored?
All logs and settings stay on your local machine.
Do I need internet?
No — Gaze works locally once installed.
How does focus tracking work?
It monitors tab switches and webcam attention signals in real time.
How does the reward system work?
Choose your focus method (Pomodoro, Count Up, Custom, etc.). You earn points every time you maintain focus and avoid distractions like tab switches, looking away, or losing face detection over the allotted time period.

Higher consistency increases your level and unlocks better ranks (egg, hatchling, fledgling, eagle) over time.
